# Does blastema onset depend on cut level?

Registered 2026-11-23 before the literature search. Frozen model 2fb26c4c5326e18ac724e11143e66568cd00959f. Trigger module. The engine recruits progenitors at wound closure. Our prediction is that proximal and distal cuts first show a blastema in the same observation interval. The catalog used eventual replacement of lost length for development. Timing of first blastema appearance across cut levels is the new contrast.

Use the mid-limb and distal-cut scenarios, seeds 0–4, 400 steps, at distal-growth thresholds 0.1, 0.2 and 0.4. For each pair record the first step with positive progenitor count. Both arms must complete faithfully. Denervated controls must have no blastema and no-termination controls must exhibit uncontrolled growth at each threshold and seed. Count missing or failed controls explicitly.

Search Europe PMC and PubMed for primary axolotl limb studies comparing proximal and distal amputation levels and blastema timing or regeneration stages. Exclude the registry and literature notes used to build this version. Prefer the earliest eligible primary publication with retrievable arm-specific observations on comparable animals, intact innervation and a common staging schedule. Check the full source for the first identified blastema at both levels. Use the first comparable scheduled assessment with a blastema in each arm (median onset bin if individual records are available). First cellular recruitment in the simulation proxies this observable. Record ascertainment method and resolution. The same onset bin supports the prediction at that resolution only. Different bins are a miss. A statement of nonsignificance, time to a later stage alone, or a pooled timeline cannot resolve it. Retain an unresolved contrast in the denominator if arm-specific observations or the source are unavailable. No model updates or second endpoint after unblinding.

A1, nerve dependence, has verified primary support. A2, A3 and A5 are modelling hypotheses. A fixed onset step is an uncalibrated timing assumption. Agreement at a sampling interval would not verify this mechanism.
