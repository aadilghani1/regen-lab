# What makes a limb stop growing?

Press **Play**. Watch the pink cells rebuild the missing part. They form a **blastema**: a group of cells at the cut that helps grow new tissue.

In this model, each new cell carries a clue about where it belongs along the limb. Growth stops at the tip. That stop rule is called **termination**.

Turn off **Termination** and press **Play** again. Watch the limb length. Does it pass the original tip?
