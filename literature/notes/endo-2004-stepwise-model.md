## Claim in one sentence

According to the abstract, a nerve deviated to a lateral skin wound induces an ectopic blastema, and adding skin from the opposite side of the limb lets that blastema keep growing into an ectopic limb, separating blastema induction (nerve) from outgrowth and patterning (positional disparity).

## Evidence

From the abstract only (no figure or section could be checked):

- Nerve deviation to a skin wound on the side of the limb induces an ectopic blastema.
- Grafting skin from the contralateral side of the limb at the same time lets the ectopic blastema continue to grow and form an ectopic limb.
- Dermal cell migration, contribution and proliferation were analysed and ectopic blastemas were judged equivalent to amputation blastemas.
- Nerve signals are required to induce both ectopic and normal blastemas; the diversity of positional information from cells of opposite sides induces outgrowth and pattern formation.
