# Maden 1982: Vitamin A and pattern formation in the regenerating limb

- Citation (full): Maden M. (1982) Vitamin A and pattern formation in the regenerating limb. Nature 295:672-675.
- DOI / PMID: 10.1038/295672a0 / PMID 7057925
- Read by / date: AI agent (Claude), 2026-11-23, abstract only; full text not read (paywalled at Nature; no abstract or PMC copy in Europe PMC; abstract read on the Nature article page)
- Organism and system: axolotl, regenerating limbs treated with vitamin A and other retinoids

## Claim in one sentence

According to the abstract, retinoids cause specific changes in the proximodistal pattern of regenerating axolotl limbs, in some cases giving a complete limb from an amputation at the carpal (wrist) level.

## Evidence

From the abstract only (no figure or section could be checked):

- Vitamin A and its derivatives altered the proximodistal pattern of regenerating axolotl limbs.
- In some cases a complete limb regrew from a carpal-level amputation, that is, proximal structures already present in the stump were formed again.
- The author frames this conditionally: if retinoids change the positional information of limb cells, they could be probes of the molecular basis of pattern formation.

## Relevance to the model

It **does not check** two parts of the statement: that the cause is an "excess proximal signal", and that the effect depends on treatment "while the blastema forms". ## Caveats

- Abstract only; full text not read, so dose-response, timing and frequency of duplication are not recorded here (and dosing is out of scope for this repository in any case).
- Effects occurred "in some cases", so proximalization is graded and incomplete, not all-or-none.
- The model represents retinoid action as an exogenous proximal signal; the abstract does not say retinoids are, or mimic, an endogenous proximal signal.
