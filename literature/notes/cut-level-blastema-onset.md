# Evidence for blastema onset at two axolotl limb cut levels

Codex, 2026-11-23. The registered contrast requires first detection on a common observation schedule, recorded separately for proximal and distal limbs. Europe PMC query: `axolotl AND proximal AND distal AND (timing OR stages) AND blastema`. Additional PubMed-indexed and full-text searches used upper arm, forearm, amputation level, early bud and blastema appearance.

| Primary study | What was measured | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|
| [Tank et al., 1976, Methods and early-bud stage](https://ambystoma.uky.edu/genetic-stock-center/documents/Tank%20et%20al%201976.pdf) | Daily morphological staging and a histological series. Both forelimbs were cut at the same upper-arm level. | One level only. |
| [Gardiner et al., 1995, Figs. 4–5](https://escholarship.org/content/qt3b97d88q/qt3b97d88q.pdf) | Early Hox expression at multiple cut levels and expression in stage-selected blastemas. | Gene-expression onset and stage-selected specimens do not give first morphological detection in both arms. |
| [Regeneration of Limb Joints, 2012, Methods and Fig. 4](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3503958/) | Multiple cut levels. The figure describes differentiation in blastemas already present by three weeks. | Presence at a later assessment leaves onset unknown. |
| [Transcriptional Correlates of Proximal-Distal Identity and Regeneration Timing, PMID 29107037, §§2–3](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5920805/) | Forearm and upper-arm transcriptional time courses. Analysis intervals derive from the forearm series. Early cell-cycle expression differs by level. | The intervals do not independently date first blastema detection in the upper arm. |

The reviewed sources leave the registered contrast unresolved. The best follow-up is a dated image or staging series with identifiable cut levels, an observation before first appearance, and a common scoring schedule. Previously inspected expression and completion-time results are ineligible as fresh held-out endpoints.
