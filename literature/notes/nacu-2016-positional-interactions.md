# Nacu 2016, positional tissue interactions and outgrowth

Nacu E, Gromberg E, Oliveira CR, Drechsel D, Tanaka EM. Nature 533, 407–410 (2016). DOI 10.1038/nature17972, PMID 27120163. Codex read the published abstract on 2026-11-23. [Primary source](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature17972)

In axolotls an anterior-only blastema can form but ordinarily regresses. The authors report that complementary tissue signals sustain outgrowth. This separates initiation from subsequent growth and motivates a comparison with an innervated blastema containing both positional identities.

A8 approximates this observation by retaining initial wound signalling and withdrawing subsequent distal signal production when posterior identity is absent. The abstract establishes neither the timing at wound closure nor the decay rate or size of the residual outgrowth. The current lattice models amputation and has no regression process. Full-text verification and the accessory-limb geometry remain open.
